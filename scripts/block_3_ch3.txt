[147] (normal) 
[148] (normal) Agricultural supply chains involve multiple stakeholders, including farmers, warehouses, logistics providers, retailers, and consumers. Tracking agricultural products throughout these stages can be challenging due to limited transparency, fragmented records, and difficulties in verifying product origin and handling conditions. Environmental changes during storage and transportation may also affect product quality. Furthermore, IoT-based monitoring systems can generate incorrect, abnormal, or repeated sensor readings. Without proper validation, such data may reduce the reliability of supply chain records. Therefore, a reliable framework is required to validate incoming telemetry data, identify suspicious readings, and support transparent product traceability. The problem addressed by this project is the development of a decentralized frame-work that integrates IoT-based monitoring, AI-driven data validation, and blockchain-supported traceability to improve the transparency, reliability, and accountability of agricultural supply chain information.
