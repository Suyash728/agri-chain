1: Introduction
[104] (normal) 1.1 Introduction
[105] (normal) Agriculture plays a vital role in providing food and supporting the economy. However, agricultural products pass through several stages before reaching consumers, including farming, storage, transportation, and retail distribution. Maintaining product quality, monitoring environmental conditions, and ensuring transparency throughout these stages can be challenging. Traditional supply chain systems may face issues such as limited traceability, data inconsistencies, and difficulties in verifying the origin and handling history of agricultural products. The proposed framework, titled “A Decentralized IoT-Blockchain Framework with Customized Smart Contracts for Transparent and Traceable Agricultural Supply Chains,” addresses these challenges by integrating the Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and blockchain technology. The framework supports the monitoring of agricultural products, validation of sensor data, and traceability of products across different supply chain stakeholders. The system considers the movement of agricultural products from farmers to ware-houses or logistics providers, dark stores or retailers, and finally to consumers. IoT devices and simulated telemetry sources provide environmental readings, such as temperature and humidity, to support the monitoring of product handling conditions. The AI-based Trust Layer evaluates incoming telemetry data to identify invalid readings, physically implausible values, unusual patterns, and repeated events before the data proceeds to the next stage.
[106] (normal) The AI Trust Layer is developed using Python and FastAPI. It incorporates schema validation, physical and temporal plausibility checks, feature extraction, Isolation Forest-based anomaly detection, SHA-256-based event fingerprinting, and replay detection. Based on these checks, the system generates a verdict indicating whether the data is valid, anomalous, or supported by insufficient evidence. Suspicious data is quarantined, while eligible data is prepared for handoff to the blockchain integration layer. The framework integrates AI-based data validation with blockchain-based records, cloud storage, and a web interface for supply chain stakeholders. Blockchain supports the traceability of product registration, ownership transfers, and supply chain events. The web interface provides access to relevant information for farmers, logistics providers, retailers, and consumers. A QRbased consumer interface facilitates access to product journey information, thereby improving visibility into the movement of agricultural products across the supply chain.
[107] (normal) 1.2 Aim of the Project
[108] (normal) The main aim of the project is to develop a decentralized IoT-blockchain framework that improves the transparency, traceability, and reliability of agricultural supply chain information through AI-based telemetry validation and blockchain-supported record management.
[109] (normal) 1.3 Scope of the Project
[110] (normal) The scope of the proposed framework covers agricultural product monitoring, telemetry validation, anomaly detection, and traceability across the supply chain. It considers different stakeholders, including farmers, warehouse or logistics operators, retailers, and consumers. The AI Trust Layer, developed using Python and FastAPI, performs telemetry schema validation, physical and temporal plausibility checks, feature extraction, machine-learning based anomaly detection, event fingerprinting, replay detection, verdict generation, quarantine handling, and Oracle handoff preparation. A telemetry simulator generates normal and faulty readings to evaluate the behavior of the system under different conditions. The framework also incorporates IoT-based monitoring, blockchain smart contracts, cloud-based storage, and a web interface for stakeholder interaction. The blockchain com-ponent supports product registration, custody tracking, and supply chain event recording. Cloud storage supports the management of relevant application data and product documents, while the user interface provides access to supply chain information. QR-based traceability enables consumers to retrieve relevant details about a product’s journey.
[111] (normal) The scope also includes testing and evaluation of the AI Trust Layer using automated tests and simulated telemetry scenarios. Together, these components provide a framework for improving data reliability, supply chain visibility, and accountability from the origin of agricultural products to the final consumer.
[112] (normal) 1.4 Significant Contributions
[113] (normal) The project integrates IoT, Artificial Intelligence, and blockchain technology into a unified framework for agricultural supply chain traceability. A major contribution is the development of an AI-based Trust Layer that validates telemetry data before it is passed to the blockchain integration layer. The AI Trust Layer combines physical and temporal plausibility checks with 11-dimensional feature extraction and Isolation Forest-based anomaly detection to identify suspicious telemetry patterns. SHA-256-based event fingerprinting and replay checks help iden-tify repeated or suspicious telemetry events. A verdict engine classifies incoming data as valid, anomalous, or supported by insufficient evidence, while quarantine and Oracle handoff mechanisms support the controlled processing of telemetry data. Another contribution is the integration of telemetry simulation and automated test-ing to evaluate the behaviour of the AI Trust Layer under normal and faulty conditions. The broader framework connects data validation with blockchain-supported product records, cloud-based storage, and a QR-enabled user interface for product traceability. These contributions demonstrate how AI-based validation can complement blockchain technology by assessing the reliability of incoming data before it is considered for traceability records. The framework aims to improve transparency, accountability, and trust among agricultural supply chain stakeholders.
