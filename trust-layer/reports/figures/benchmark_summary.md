## Scientific Notes & Evaluation Scope
SCIENTIFIC CAVEATS & EVALUATION SCOPE:
1. Benchmark environment: Evaluated on synthetic, controlled IoT telemetry streams generated by the Phase 9 simulator.
2. Scope distinction: Detection rates reflect performance against simulated fault models (thermal rate spikes, humidity leaps, Haversine displacement jumps, chronological regressions, exact SHA-256 replays, and telemetry gaps). They do NOT represent empirical field guarantees for real-world agricultural IoT sensors.
3. ML state: Isolation Forest uses development hyperparameters (contamination=0.05, n_estimators=100) fitted to synthetic normal route baselines.
4. Execution state: Evaluated using in-memory development repositories without persistent database latency.
5. Boundary guarantees: Only VALID + READY_FOR_ORACLE events are eligible for Phase 10 downstream Oracle handoff. Anomalous records remain quarantined off-chain.
