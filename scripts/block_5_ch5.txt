[180] (normal) 5.1 AI Trust Layer Performance Evaluation
[181] (normal) The AI Trust Layer evaluates incoming IoT telemetry before it is considered for blockchain recording. The evaluation documented in the project repository uses 1,000 synthetic sensor transmissions covering seven scenarios: normal readings, thermal spikes, refrigeration failure, sensor freeze, calibration drift, GPS deviations, and replay attacks. The Isolation Forest model uses an 11-dimensional feature vector to identify unusual telemetry patterns. Physical plausibility checks, crop-specific policy rules, and SHA-256-based replay detection complement the machine-learning model. The recorded evaluation metrics are presented in Table 5.1.

[229] (normal) 5.6 Overall Discussion
[230] (normal) The recorded evaluation combines AI performance measurements, automated tests, end-to-end verification, and smart-contract gas benchmarks. The AI evaluation uses 1,000 synthetic samples and reports 97.20% accuracy, 96.30% precision, 98.18% recall, an F1-score of 0.9722, and a mean inference latency of 1.84 ms per reading. The recorded test suite contains 147 passing tests. 
[231] (normal) The gas benchmark reports a reduction of 52.71% for batched recording of 20 environmental condition readings. Together, these results demonstrate the intended contribution of combining AI-based telemetry validation with blockchain-supported traceability. The Trust Layer assesses in-coming data before it is considered for blockchain recording, while modular smart contracts manage product registration, custody changes, and validated condition records. 
[232] (normal) Cloud storage and decentralized document storage support application records and product documentation, and the web interface provides supply chain stakeholders with access to relevant information.
[233] (normal) The AI metrics are based on synthetic telemetry, and the gas figures describe the documented benchmark scenario. These results should therefore be interpreted within the conditions of the reported tests and benchmarks rather than as evidence of performance across all real-world agricultural supply chains.
